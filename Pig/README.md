# Pig

A classic dice game of luck and risk (push-your-luck). Try to reach the target score before your opponent without losing your points to a bad roll!

## How to play

1. **Roll the Die:** On your turn, roll a standard six-sided die as many times as you like.
2. **Build Your Turn Bank:** Each roll adds to your temporary score for the current turn.
3. **Hold or Risk:** 
   - Choose **Hold** to lock in your turn's points and add them to your total score. Your turn ends.
   - Choose to **Roll Again** to try to score more points, but beware...
4. **The Trap (Rolling a 1):** If you roll a **1**, you lose all points accumulated in the current turn, and your turn ends immediately. (Your previously saved total score remains safe).
5. **Winning:** The first player to reach **100 points** wins the game.
